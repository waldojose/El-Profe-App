How it works

§ 01 — Process
01

Write

Open a co-writing session and work on the same lyric in real time, verse by verse.

02

Log contributions

El Profe tracks which character each writer typed — the objective basis for negotiating the split.

03

Propose a split

Drag the percentages between writers until the split feels fair to everyone.

04

Sign and export

A DocuSign-style binding signature and a split sheet PDF ready for your performing rights organization.

Features

Everything a songwriter needs to write as a team and stay protected on paper.

01 · Co-writing

Write at the same time

Live sessions with per-writer cursors, so no one steps on a verse or loses an idea in a chat.

02 · Traceability

Character-level tracking

Every word stays attributed to its author. Clear evidence when it's time to split.

03 · Manual splits

The percentages, yours

No algorithm decides your split. You negotiate, you adjust, you decide.

04 · Signatures

Binding signatures

Every writer signs their own split sheet with legal validity, just like DocuSign.

05 · Versions

Full history

Go back to any earlier draft. Nothing gets lost between the idea and the final version.

06 · Dictionary

Bilingual rhymes

Search rhymes and synonyms in Spanish and English without leaving the lyric you're writing.
