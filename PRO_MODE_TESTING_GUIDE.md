## 📈 PERCENTAGES EXPLAINED

### How It Works

1. **Character Tracking**
   - Every keystroke logged per user
   - Insertions counted as positive
   - Deletions counted as negative
   - Net characters = Added - Deleted

2. **Percentage Calculation**
   - User's net chars ÷ Total chars × 100
   - Shown with 1 decimal place
   - Updates automatically

3. **Use Case**
   - Reference data for split negotiations
   - Shows relative contribution levels
   - NOT legal ownership
   - Writers decide actual splits

### Example from Demo Song

```
Total Characters: 1012

Sarah Johnson (Writer)
- Added: 370 chars
- Deleted: 15 chars
- Net: 355 chars
- Percentage: 35.1%

Michael Chen (Producer)
- Net: 325 chars
- Percentage: 32.1%

Emily Rodriguez (Composer)
- Net: 332 chars
- Percentage: 32.8%
```

**Note:** Percentages shown are for contribution tracking only. The actual split proposal is 40/35/25, decided manually by the writers.
